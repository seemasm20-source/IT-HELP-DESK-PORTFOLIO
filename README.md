## 🧭 How I Troubleshoot

1. **Listen** to the user, and confirm the symptom.
2. **Verify** who is asking before I change an account.
3. **Isolate** the layer: the account, the licence, the app, the network or the service.
4. **Test from the inside out**, for example `ipconfig`, then the gateway, then the internet, then DNS.
5. **Fix the simplest cause first**, and change one thing at a time.
6. **Confirm with the user**, then **document** the cause, the fix and the result.
7. **Escalate in time**, with the evidence collected, when the SLA is at risk.
